## Policy

Start at R0=14, R1=16. Predict R* from the decay rate only if w1 < 0.5·w0 (otherwise step +2), capped at R1+30. Verify; step +2 on failure. Every attempt is charged (both bounds). Search limit R=40. Specialist: full DOP853 rtol 3e-10, the R-036 specialist counts.

## Reading

- Four of six cases resolve. The predict-then-verify policy cuts the charged cost in the two cases it previously over-spent (seed 7 center 256: 0.83 → 0.40; center 100: 0.99 → 0.49).
- Seed 99 center 256 resolves only at R=34 and costs 1.84× the specialist, so the envelope is worse than the specialist there.
- Seed 20261014 center 100 is unresolved at R≤40.
- These are per-case charged ratios on six cases. Specialist accuracy is not exactly matched (8e-11 to 9.9e-10), so the ratios are rough.

## Limits

Non-rigorous. Six cases, one forcing family, specialist accuracy not exactly matched, single-agent review.

## Decision

The envelope is not a general cost win over full-domain DOP853 in this family. A case-dependent envelope with a DOP853 fallback is the only defensible use; the fallback costs the full specialist in the unresolved cases. PARK, no MAP trigger.
