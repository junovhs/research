## Reading

- Charging failed attempts removes the favorable ratio. Where the envelope succeeds, it costs 0.33–0.99 of the matched-tolerance specialist.
- Two of six cases do not reach 1e-9 by R=32, so the envelope gives no certified answer there and would need the specialist anyway.
- **Retraction:** the 0.070 "matched" ratio from R-031 (and the 0.117 from R-030) is not a general figure. It came from one case without charged attempts.
- Specialist errors here (8e-11 to 9.9e-10) are not exactly matched to the envelope widths; the ratio is a rough comparison, not a matched-accuracy measurement.

## Limits

Non-rigorous. Six cases, one forcing family. Specialist accuracy not exactly matched. Single-agent review.

## Decision

Envelope is not a general cost win over full-domain DOP853 in this family. Keep as a case-dependent option only, with a fallback. No MAP trigger.
