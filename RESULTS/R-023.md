## Discrete equations and exact derivation

Let matrices M^F_t, M^P_t be nonnegative row-stochastic, D-by-D, and let C,F,S,P be D-vectors. Transport first:

    Cbar=M^F_t C_t+b_t, Fbar=M^F_t F_t,
    Sbar=M^F_t S_t, Pbar=M^P_t P_t.

With per-site nonlinear extents u=Cbar*Fbar/64 and v=Cbar*Sbar/128, update:

    C'=Cbar-u-v; F'=Fbar-u; S'=Sbar-v;
    P'=Pbar+y(u+v),   y=1/4.

This is an **exact discrete recurrence**. For Z=P+y(F+S) and E_t=M^F_t-M^P_t, cancellation of both arbitrary reaction extents gives

    Z_{t+1}=M^P_t Z_t+y E_t(F_t+S_t).                 (1)

Let w_N=e_j, w_t=(M^P_t)^T w_{t+1} and g_t=E_t^T w_{t+1}. Telescoping (1) yields

    P_{N,j}=w_0^T Z_0+y sum_{t=0}^{N-1}g_t^T(F_t+S_t)
            -y(F_{N,j}+S_{N,j}).                    (2)

For different F and S transports, replace the one E with separate E^F and E^S. Equation (2) is exact for every nonlinear trajectory satisfying the reaction stoichiometry; it is an ordinary linear adjoint applied to a defect source, **not** an approximation of a nonlinear adjoint. In particular the previous R-019 passive mode is recovered for E=0.

**Conditional input-access lemma.** Treat the port values F_t and S_t as arbitrary independently accessible scalar input fields at each time, with no other trusted aggregates. Computing the linear correction in (2) exactly requires inspecting every coordinate i for which g_{t,i} is nonzero, otherwise perturbing only that unread coordinate by delta changes the correction by y*g_{t,i}*delta. This is a lower bound for the *port interface*, NOT for the reachable state manifold of the full nonlinear dynamics, which may couple those values. The relevant sparsity metric is sum_t nnz(E_t^T w_{t+1}), **not rank(E_t)**. If E changes k rows of width at most s, nnz(g_t)<=k*s. A rank-one row change with dense column support can nevertheless force nnz(g_t)=D.
