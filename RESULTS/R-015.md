# R-015 — Phase-conditioned continuous-time tail: decision gate

**Date:** 2026-10-13. **Parent:** [R-014](R-014.md). **EXPLORATORY: YES. PREREGISTERED: NO. POD: NO. Evidence: REPORTED. NOVELTY UNVERIFIED.** No independent challenger; review is one-agent self-review only.

## Target, alternatives, and decision

Question: for the *semidiscrete continuous-time* Allen–Cahn ODE, can an independently supplied phase bit justify forgetting most of a remote initial field, while retaining a recent forcing pulse? This is **not** the continuum PDE, a verified finite-arithmetic solver, or a practical industrial query yet. The alternative is a validated high-order full-state ODE integrator with a goal-oriented bound; a different mechanism elsewhere in the lab may have higher value than further tuning. **Decision: PARK** building a general certified Allen–Cahn solver now; **CONTINUE only a small phase-aware, suffix-forcing error-bound falsifier** if a realistic workload and specialist comparator are identified. No broad performance claim.

For D=16 periodic sites and d=1, evolve `u_i' = d(u_(i-1)-2u_i+u_(i+1)) + u_i-u_i^3+b(t)`. The input consists of unknown heterogeneous initial values in either [0.5,2]^D or [-2,-0.5]^D, and a known uniform drive b=0 up to t=10.5 followed by b=0 or -0.25 until t=11.5. The desired output is one terminal site at absolute accuracy 1e-9. A full continuous-time solve from t=0 (or a validated enclosure method with comparable guarantees) is the relevant comparator; no cost evaluation was run.

## Analytic argument and assumptions

Both sign-boxes are invariant for |b|<=3/8. For unforced scalar endpoints x in a fixed phase, the exact reaction flow is
`F_t(x)=sign(x)/sqrt(1+(x^-2-1)*exp(-2t))`.
The cooperative diffusion system preserves componentwise bounds by spatially constant scalar solutions. At t=10.5, the extremal positive scalar endpoint values are 0.999999998862616 and 1.000000000284346; negative values are their sign reversals. For t in [10.5,11.5], scalar forced endpoint solutions again sandwich every heterogeneous site (comparison principle). On these scalar phase ranges, `f'(u)=1-3u^2` is negative and bounded above by approximately -0.92 during the tested -0.25 pulse; a **conservative contraction-rate assumption 0.92 was used**, not verified with directed-rounding interval arithmetic in this probe. The resulting width estimate is 5.665865029851688e-10. Do **not** call that a machine-certified bound: input rounding, scalar integration error and a rigorous proof that the chosen derivative bound holds across the entire trajectory are outstanding.
