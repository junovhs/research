## A much closer guarantee-oriented water-quality specialist predates R-018

The original R-018 report treated **Shang, Uber and Polycarpou (2002)** particle backtracking and a water-network adjoint as its closest specialists. That missed a still stronger *output-specific uncertainty-bounding* result:

- **Vrachimis, Eliades and Polycarpou (2021)**, *Calculating Chlorine Concentration Bounds in Water Distribution Networks: A Backtracking Uncertainty Bounding Approach*, *Water Resources Research*, **DOI [10.1029/2020WR028684](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020WR028684)**. **Read primary article**, including introduction and §§3,5. This Backtracking Uncertainty Bounding Algorithm (BUBA) already backtracks selected chlorine-sensor output paths, excludes unrelated network regions, and builds bounds with uncertain hydraulics and time-varying bulk/pipe reaction parameters. The authors show ~30,000 Monte Carlo benchmark checks on Hanoi and CY-DMA networks and a real Cyprus network use case with online flow/quality estimation. The paper has a 2015 prior conference version (DOI 10.1016/j.proeng.2015.08.914). BUBA's sensor-output path pruning and bounded concentration are **directly relevant to R-013/R-014-style promises of sparse water sensor certificates**, even beyond the linear adjoint tested in R-018.
- **Adjoint-based error control for the simulation and optimization of gas and water supply networks (2015)**, DOI [10.1016/j.amc.2015.03.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amc.2015.03.029), has goal-oriented model/discretization-error control, a stronger application-level comparator than naive discrete Euler replay for physical-network outputs.
- **Elsherif et al. (2023)**, [*Comprehensive Framework for Controlling Nonlinear Multi-Species Water Quality Dynamics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10104), explores nonlinear multispecies state models and model-order reduction, so simply moving from linear to nonlinear chemistry **does not** automatically establish a distinct specialist gap. The [EPA EPANET-MSX manual](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P10188CK.TXT) documents precomputed hydraulics reuse, but input-record random access and per-scenario cost remain separate unresolved obligations.

**Critical distinctions:** BUBA's claimed bounds address uncertainties in physical hydraulic and reaction parameters, not automatically a verified directed-rounding enclosure of a precisely specified fixed, rational, discrete recurrence; BUBA may make different mixing/flow and partial-observation assumptions. Thus this is an **overlap and stronger competitor**, **not** a proved equality between methods, a formal logical refutation of R-013, or a new result. Conversely R-018's exact rational transpose identity and toy timing **do not** establish a physical-network numerical-certificate capability missing from BUBA, much less prove industrial gain. Prior art must be implemented/matched and all input preprocessing cost charged before any promotion.

**Revised water candidate decision:** reinforce **PARK** for generic linear and uncertainty-bounded chlorine-sensor backtracking, rather than undertaking a new R-013/R-014 reconstruction on this application. An externally documented output/accuracy contract **and** a specific capability outside BUBA and the established goal-oriented/numerical specialist set would be necessary.
