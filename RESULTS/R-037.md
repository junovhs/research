## Defect

The policy estimated the per-site decay rate κ = ln(w0/w1)/(R1−R0) for every case. For the two saturated cases, w0 and w1 were both 2.0 up to rounding, so κ was a rounding-level number. That predicted a radius far beyond the search limit (R=40), so no further attempts ran and those cases were reported as unresolved. **Their "none" results are an artifact of this defect, not of the envelope.**
