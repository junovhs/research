## Reading

The cap cuts the envelope cost by about 65% (1.88e6 → 6.6e5) with width and containment unchanged, so the envelope itself is accuracy-insensitive to the cap at this tolerance. The ratio moves only from 0.1445 to 0.140 (about 3%). **That 3% is not a clean matched-cost win:** the full-domain specialist's own error rises from 1.9e-9 to 2.5e-9 as its cost falls, so its cost drop is partly accuracy loss. A fair ratio needs the specialist at matched accuracy; that is not established here.

## Limits

Non-rigorous. Single seeded forcing family and one center. Single-agent review.

## Decision

Use max_step=1.0 for the envelope (accuracy-neutral here, 65% cheaper). The specialist comparison stays at the max_step=0.125 accuracy (ratio 0.1445) until a matched-accuracy specialist run exists.
