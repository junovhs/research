## Decision gate: a real workload, output and strongest alternative

**Demand and observable.** Fisher (2023), [*Integrated EPANET-MSX process models of chlorine and its by-products in drinking water distribution systems*](https://doi.org/10.1002/wer.10949), derives chlorine dosing strategies for the *real Mirrabooka pipeline* by varying the initial and booster dose. The paper explicitly targets **chlorine ≥0.2 mg/L at outlet node E**, with associated THM/HAA byproduct constraints. It reports an optimal dose of 0.76 mg/L at 30 °C for single injection versus 0.43 + 0.26 mg/L with a booster, and observes that even the simple application needs numerous simulator evaluations. This is a credible repeated *few-terminal-observable* design subproblem. **However, the real utility-wide safety requirement is not generally one-output:** both Fisher's formulation and [2024 EPANET-MSX optimization](https://doi.org/10.2166/hydro.2024.090) consider chlorine/byproducts throughout a distribution system, and the latter uses constraints at **all junctions and time steps**. The few-output contract is conditional and has not been established as the production optimizer's whole workload.

**Inputs/access.** The [EPA EPANET-MSX manual](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P10188CK.TXT) permits `MSXusehydfile` to reuse a previously saved hydraulics file. Thus a *fixed-hydraulics scenario ensemble* has plausible input reuse; hydraulic preparation, water-quality source histories, and retrieving internal time-indexed mixing coefficients remain real costs that were **not measured** in this experiment. No Mirrabooka raw hydraulics or kinetic input file was executed here. The numerical research object below is a **distinct explicit discrete toy** with heterogeneity and synthetic schedules, not any version of EPANET-MSX.

**Strongest genuine specialist.** The reaction invariant is not new: left-nullspace/stoichiometric conservation modes are established, e.g. [Famili and Palsson (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0006-3495(03)74450-6), and [Fisher's Table 2](https://doi.org/10.1002/wer.10949) itself explicitly relates byproduct formation to fast/slow reactant consumption. Backward transport of sensor information was established by [Shang, Uber and Polycarpou (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)0733-9372(2002)128:5(441)); [Vrachimis et al. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020WR028684) already bound chlorine sensor outputs by backtracking uncertain flows and reaction rates, in real networks; and [2024 property-preserving passive-tracer transport](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.camwa.2024.02.031) explicitly treats preservation of linear tracer relationships. A **well optimized conservation-reduced solver** is therefore the relevant specialist, not a naive full four-species Euler interpreter. This run implements that specialist algebra, so a speed ratio versus the naive interpreter cannot be called a new specialist-relative advantage.

**Decision-changing question and falsifier.** Can the 2RA/THM/HAA reaction structure be reorganized so that *one byproduct output* needs no byproduct trajectory and can reuse a single passive adjoint across different dosing scenarios? If exact rational forward and transformed outputs disagree, the construction fails. If it requires equal transport, constant yields, or a full nonlinear species replay in all cases (or the strongest rival already performs the same reduction), it cannot independently justify new R-013/R-014 engineering. Opportunity cost: prior R-018 and the more general nonlinear reaction/goal-oriented specialists outrank tuning this easy synthetic gain.

## Exact discrete equations, output and derivation

For D periodic cells, let the time-dependent scalar linear transport operator applied identically to each transported field be

    (M_t v)_i = a_t v_i + l_t v_{i-1} + r_t v_{i+1},
    a_t=5/8 + ((t mod 7)-3)/512,
    l_t=1/4 - ((t mod 7)-3)/512,  r_t=1/8.

Every coefficient is nonnegative and `a_t+l_t+r_t=1` for all t. Let `(C,F,S,P)` denote chlorine, two bulk reactant groups, and one lumped chemical byproduct. At each time step first mix all four fields by `M_t`, add a chlorine-only dose `b_{t,i}` to the mixed chlorine, and set, with mixed quantities denoted by a bar,

    u_i = h k_f Cbar_i Fbar_i,       v_i = h k_s Cbar_i Sbar_i,
    C_i' = Cbar_i - u_i - v_i - h k_w Cbar_i,
    F_i' = Fbar_i - u_i,            S_i' = Sbar_i - v_i,
    P_i' = Pbar_i + y(u_i+v_i).

These are a *chosen explicit Euler/splitting recurrence* with bulk 2R/2RA-like kinetics plus an independently modeled chlorine wall sink. **The reported target is the exact mathematical output of this recurrence**, `(C_N,41,P_N,41)`, not a continuous physical system, a fixed-IVP bound, a regulated concentration, or a real EPANET output. Constants for the floating experiment: `D=96, N=1024, h=1/8, k_f=0.12, k_s=0.025, k_w=0.01, y=0.23`, with heterogeneous, seeded, bounded initial states and 24 independently generated six-window chlorine dosage schedules; full config/source and seeds are archived. The injection sites are chosen from `[3,19,54,73]` and dosing amplitudes from a seeded finite menu; input-event construction is charged on both sides of each benchmark, but disk/hydraulic access is excluded. The 1/8 step has **no assigned physical time unit**.

**Exact reaction invariant.** Define the transformed species

    Z_i = P_i + y(F_i+S_i).

Because the two reaction losses in F,S equal the two stoichiometric contributions to P, including when chlorine separately decays at the wall, the reaction substep satisfies `Z_i' = Pbar_i+y(Fbar_i+Sbar_i)`. Identical *linear* transport yields `Z_{t+1}=M_t Z_t` even though C,F,S evolve nonlinearly and vary with each chlorine-only dosage scenario. For one terminal site `j`, define an **independent** backward linear functional `w_N=e_j, w_t=M_t^T w_{t+1}`. Therefore,

    P_{N,j} = (w_0^T Z_0) - y(F_{N,j}+S_{N,j}).

This exact identity eliminates the P field's time evolution entirely. If `Z_0` and all `M_t` are scenario-shared, the passive output `w_0^T Z_0` can be prepared once and reused across **all dosage schedules**, but **the nonlinear trajectories of C,F,S still must be calculated**. This is a source-conditional recombination of **already established** conservation plus adjoint methods, not a new physical chemistry law or a claim that a unique general shortcut exists. The result is exact in real/rational arithmetic only; comparing floating implementations does not certify finite arithmetic. With independent source injections into F,S,P, one needs the adjoint-weighted source sum as in R-018; source-specific yields require separate conservative modes. Claiming `Z` to be passive when any species has a different transport operator is invalid.

**Structural hostile cases:** if the *actual* byproduct yield changes between steps, a fixed `y` no longer cancels the reaction increment; or if byproducts are formed at the pipe wall independently of depletion in F/S, the cancellation also fails. The tested exact rational variable-yield difference was `-1/1600` for one scalar step (actual y=1/5, assumed y=23/100). An intentionally unaccounted wall byproduct contribution `1/1024` independently violates the invariant. These are counterexamples to the **specific simplification**, not to full multispecies simulation; a more elaborate stoichiometric transform might still work if the extra reactions are represented.

## Decision, strongest rival and lineage test

**Decision: PARK** the invariant reduction as a new stand-alone performance research line. The extra chemistry variable can be eliminated in this restricted model, but the reduction is a direct consequence of published stoichiometric conservation laws and the source paper's byproduct relationship; R-019 did not identify a novel gap or prove a desirable strong-specialist advantage. The important completed boundary is **which pieces of a nonlinear reactive model can be pulled back exactly as passive coordinates** and which must still be solved. This *does not* validate the actual multi-species hydraulic model, prove any 0.2 mg/L dose is safe, or warrant pursuing yet another synthetic window improvement.

**Opportunity cost and falsifier for reopening:** a genuinely external real fixed-hydraulics dataset with scenario-input/read contracts, a task requiring few terminal species outputs (as opposed to all-node-all-time protection), required numerical/model accuracy, and an optimized stoichiometric-reduced chemical specialist must be available. Only continue if an additional input-specific *verified sparse F/S component* provides new utility **after charging all cold/setup, failed admission/fallback and hydraulic/data access cost**. Alternatively investigate another mathematically different mechanism rather than tuning reactor yields. The stronger exact discrete output problem and independent reviewer remain open.

**MAP decision:** no update. This is an instance of *known* reaction invariants + already published linear adjoints, not evidence of a distinct, specialist-relative research lineage, decisive closure of general R-013, or a new established enabling theorem. It would be misleading to insert an edge claiming an R-013→R-019 implementation (none was built) solely because this report exists. **Challenge:** only the author's exact arithmetic and alternate implementation, no distinct reviewer or external scientific replication. Status stays **REPORTED**, not REPRODUCED or CERTIFIED.
