## Reading

- **The seed-20261014, center-256 case is the only one with a valid width ≤1e-9 at R=18.** The R-031 and R-032 settings were tuned to it, so they are not general.
- **Containment fails at rtol 1e-7** in three cases, with widths near 1e-6. The patch solve's own error is larger than the target at that tolerance; R-035 confirms this (see below).
- Two cases have width ≈2.0 at R=18, meaning the exterior bound saturates and the enclosure is uninformative at that radius.

## Limits

Non-rigorous. Six cases, one forcing family. Single-agent review.

## Decision

The R-031 and R-032 conclusion ("patch rtol 1e-7 and R=18 are the settings") is **retracted as a general result**. See [R-035](R-035.md) and [R-036](R-036.md).
